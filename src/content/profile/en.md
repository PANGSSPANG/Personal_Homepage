I am a backend engineer with five years of experience in e-commerce transactions and internal productivity tools. I care less about piling up features and more about whether a flow can be seen clearly: can we locate a failure, and can we verify a change?

For the last two years I have worked on settlement and reconciliation: payment gateways, order state machines, refunds and split payments. My habit is to draw the boundaries first and write code second. If a diagram can explain the structure, I do not rush into abstraction.

Outside work I build small tools and write project retrospectives. This site collects that material so I can look back on it, and so anyone who needs to know what I do can skim it quickly.
